Questions

Does Bash take a cut of my gifts or shagun?

No. Guests pay you directly through their own UPI app. That money never passes through Bash or Razorpay, and we charge nothing on it.

What happens to Plus if I delete my Bash?

Plus is tied to that one Bash, so it ends when the Bash is deleted. See our refund policy for when you can get your money back.

Do guests ever pay Bash?

Never. Viewing a list, reserving a gift and sending shagun are always free for guests.
