    Questions

    How does the gift finder work?

    You answer five quick questions about the person: who they are to you, how they spend weekends, what kind of gift lands best, what they’re into, and your budget. It matches those answers against a hand-picked list of gifts that work well in India and shows the six best fits.

    Is the gift finder free?

    Yes, the quiz and its results are free, with no sign-up.

    Can I use it to build my own wishlist?

    Yes. Choose “Me, for my wishlist” as the first answer, tick the ideas you like, and add them to your Bash in one go.

    Are the prices exact?

    No. They’re rough prices to help you stay in budget. Check the current price before you buy.
