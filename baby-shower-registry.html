Tips for a baby registry

  • Add sizes beyond newborn. Babies outgrow the first size in weeks.
  • Put the expensive essentials up as chip-in gifts so nobody feels they have to cover them alone.
  • Share the link with the invite for the godh bharai or shower, and again before the naming ceremony.

Questions

What should I put on a baby registry?

A mix of everyday things like swaddles and clothes, a few mid-range items like a carrier or monitor, and one or two big items, like the pram, that relatives can chip in on.

Can relatives give money instead?

Yes. Turn on the shagun fund and they can send any amount with their blessings, straight to your UPI.

Does it work for a naming ceremony?

Yes. Pick Baby shower as the occasion and rename it to whatever you’re celebrating.
