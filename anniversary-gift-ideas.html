Planning a group anniversary gift

  • Decide the gift first, then share. A clear goal like “Goa trip, ₹60,000” fills faster than a vague collection.
  • Turn on chip-in for the big gift so people can give what they’re comfortable with.
  • Add a short quiz about the couple for guests to play. It’s a good icebreaker at the party.

Questions

What’s a good gift for parents’ 25th anniversary?

A trip, a pilgrimage or one meaningful upgrade at home tend to work best, especially when the whole family chips in.

How do we collect money for a group gift?

Add the gift as a chip-in gift on your Bash. Everyone sends part of the price by UPI and the progress bar updates as they pay.

Is the chip-in feature free?

Chip-in gifts are part of Bash Plus, ₹149 one-time for that Bash. Lists, reservations and shagun are free.
