Housewarming list tips

  • Keep the list short and practical; guests at a griha pravesh tend to bring smaller gifts.
  • Turn on the shagun fund. Cash is the traditional housewarming gift in many families.
  • Share the list with the invite so people don’t shop before they see it.

Questions

What is a good housewarming gift in India?

Something for the new home that the hosts will actually use, like bedsheets, kitchenware, plants or an appliance. Cash shagun is also common, especially for a griha pravesh.

Is it okay to share a housewarming gift list?

Yes, especially if guests ask what you need. Including a shagun option keeps it relaxed for people who prefer cash.

Can guests send shagun by UPI?

Yes. Turn on the shagun fund and it goes straight to your UPI ID, with no cut taken.
