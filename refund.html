Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Bash Plus

Bash Plus is a one-time payment of ₹149 for a single Bash. It is not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel and you will never be charged again.

  • Within 7 days of paying: if you’re not happy, email us and we’ll refund you in full, no questions asked.
  • After 7 days: we’ll refund you if Plus features didn’t work because of a problem on our side that we couldn’t fix.
  • Charged twice, or charged but Plus didn’t unlock: email us and we’ll fix it or refund the extra payment, whenever it happened.

Deleting a Bash does not automatically trigger a refund. If you delete it within 7 days of paying, email us and we’ll refund you.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@bashit.in with the payment ID on your Razorpay receipt and the name of your Bash. Approved refunds are processed through Razorpay to your original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days depending on your bank.

Gifts and cash sent to hosts

Shagun and chip-in payments go directly from guests to hosts by UPI. Bash never receives this money, so we can’t refund it. If you sent money by mistake, please contact the host directly. Gifts bought from shops through links on a Bash are covered by that shop’s own return and refund policy.
