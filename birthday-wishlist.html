Questions

How do I share a birthday wishlist?

Make a Bash, add a few gifts and tap the WhatsApp button. Friends open the link without signing up and reserve what they’re getting.

Can friends see who is buying what?

Guests only see that a gift is taken, not who took it, so surprises stay surprises. You can choose to see who claimed what from your edit page.

Can I make a wishlist for my child’s birthday?

Yes. Many parents use it so classmates’ families know what to get and gifts don’t repeat.
